# Approved Blender study

Stimulus version: `blender-v3-20260919`. All renders were approved by the author on 19 September 2026. Each anonymous method result contains the approved Camera view above the Spatial (Global) view. The 30 fps source frames, duration, and per-view resolution are preserved; H.264 CRF 24 compression is used for delivery. No ground-truth result is shown.

Each participant evaluates 20 given-Human and 20 joint clips, with 160 separate judgments. Given-Human opponents are CCD, DanceCamera3D and DIRECTOR-C; joint opponents are PulpMotion DiT and MAR. The 48 assignment slots balance task order, opponent and left/right placement. The task transition and each trial state what to judge. Chinese and English remain switchable throughout.

The v2 question and response protocol is retained, including separate ties and inability to judge, synchronized playback, 90% watch coverage, resumable answers and idempotent submission. Browser storage is separated from the old-render questionnaire. Old response records and immutable stimulus snapshots remain private and unchanged; analyses separate stimulus versions. Automated submissions are explicitly test-only and excluded from human statistics.

The tutorial provides brief viewing and scoring instructions; it is not a video-based practice trial or evidence of a completed human pilot. Human participant results are not available until actual collection and response review.

The 20 September compact layout uses equally wide video and rating sections. Desktop ratings form a matrix with a shared six-option header and one row per criterion; each criterion expands to show its explanation. Both videos preserve their aspect ratio and fit the available viewport height. Chinese and English each show the selected language's prompt; the language switch retains the current answers. This presentation revision removes repeated cautionary wording without changing question IDs, preference values, allocation, watch requirements, or scoring.

The GitHub Pages address is stable. The existing Gradio public tunnel is temporary and must be renewed before its one-week expiry; the questionnaire frontend configuration must then be updated. Private response data, credentials and method-to-video mappings are never published to GitHub.
